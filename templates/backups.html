Backups

Two ways back

Everything this app knows lives in one database file. A snapshot gets you back from a mistake; a downloaded copy gets you back from losing the disk. They are not the same thing.

A snapshot on this disk is not an off-site backup. It protects against a bad purge or a wrong import. If the disk itself goes, the snapshots go with it — so download one now and again, and keep it somewhere else.
