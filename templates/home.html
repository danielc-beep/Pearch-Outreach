How it works

Prospect, score, draft, send.

Four steps, and the database gets better every time you run one.

1

Prospect

Search a trade and a town. Google Places, a CSV you already own, or sample data while you try it out.

2

Enrich

It visits each website for an email, a phone number, socials and what the business actually does.

3

Score

Everything is ranked 0–100 against your ideal customer profile, so the list sorts itself.

4

Reach out

Drafts are written per business, reviewed by a human, and only then sent — with unsubscribe built in.
